# Code of Conduct

Replicate This exists to make revisiting research a normal, shared, and collegial
part of science. Everyone who takes part agrees to uphold this code.

## Our commitments

- **Focus on the work, not the people.** Discuss findings, methods, and evidence —
  never the character, competence, or motives of researchers.
- **Assume good faith.** Original authors, nominators, and replicators are all
  contributing to a healthier scientific record.
- **Be constructive and specific.** Frame concerns as questions worth answering,
  and be precise about what a second look would add.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not attempt to identify, contact, or expose other users
  outside the channels they have chosen to share.

## Not permitted

- Allegations of misconduct, fraud, or research integrity violations. Those belong
  with the relevant institution or journal, not here.
- Harassment, personal attacks, or dismissive language toward any researcher.
- Nominations intended to single out, pressure, or embarrass individuals.

## Moderation

Maintainers review nominations and contributions for adherence to this code.
Content that targets people rather than evidence may be edited or removed, and a
neutral reason will be shared with the nominator.

By nominating or contributing, you confirm that you have read and agree to this
Code of Conduct.
